How I think

I'm interested in the gap between what people intend to do and what they actually do.

People ignore recommendations, abandon products, work around clunky processes, and develop habits that make perfect sense once you understand the context. I like looking beyond the obvious explanation.

My approach is a mix of curiosity, evidence, and a fairly strong urge to make things useful.

Look closer

The first explanation is rarely the whole story. I want to understand the context, friction, and small decisions shaping what happens.

Follow the evidence

Intuition is a good place to start asking questions. It is a terrible place to stop. I try to distinguish what the evidence supports from what I merely expected to find.

Make it useful

An insight becomes more interesting when it changes a decision, a design, a workflow, or the way someone understands a problem.

Think across boundaries

People, products, data, and operations affect one another. Some of the best questions emerge in the gaps between teams and disciplines.

Stay curious

I like learning new methods, challenging my own assumptions, and following a question far enough to discover that it was a slightly different question all along.
